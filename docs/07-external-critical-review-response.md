# External Critical Review Response — 2026-08-13

Status: **research-program review note, not an ADR**

The review identified a real strategic risk: we may become increasingly sophisticated at maintaining persistent compiled knowledge before demonstrating that it earns lifecycle value over raw evidence plus retrieval.

The project therefore treats the next major question as architecture-neutral:

> Under what workloads, if any, does a persistent LLM-derived knowledge layer produce greater long-horizon net value than raw evidence plus retrieval, and what is the minimum trustworthy form when such a layer is justified?

E009A completed before this change in critical path. The next planned gate is Phase 4 / Issue #15, not E009B.

The Value Gate should use staged falsification, strong raw baselines, paired scenarios, lifecycle cost, positive reuse utility, explicit uncertainty, and kill/pivot criteria. A negative result is a successful research outcome.

Detailed representation optimization remains deferred until persistent compilation demonstrates a credible region of advantage.
